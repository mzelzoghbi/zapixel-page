Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is ZaPixel?

ZaPixel is a free wallpaper app for iPhone and Android, made by ZaPlatform. It offers a curated catalogue of 4K wallpapers in categories such as nature, abstract, dark, space and minimal, with a new wallpaper hand-picked every day. You can preview any wallpaper behind a lock screen before you save it.

Is ZaPixel free?

Yes. ZaPixel is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, and every wallpaper in the catalogue is free at full 4K resolution. There is no subscription, no Pro tier and no locked content. The apps are paid for by ads, and you never need to create an account.

Which devices does ZaPixel work on?

ZaPixel runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and on Android phones through Google Play. Both apps share the same wallpaper catalogue but are built separately: the iPhone app uses a glass design with a floating tab bar, and the Android app takes its colours from your current wallpaper.

How do I set a ZaPixel wallpaper on iPhone?

Open a wallpaper, preview it on the lock screen, home screen or both, then tap Save to Photos. iOS lets no third-party app set your wallpaper directly, so open the image in Photos, tap Share and choose Use as Wallpaper. The Shortcuts app can also change it for you every day.

How do I set a ZaPixel wallpaper on Android?

Open any wallpaper in ZaPixel and tap Set. Android lets apps apply a wallpaper directly, so there is no need to save it to your gallery first. On recent Android versions the system then builds its colour palette from the new wallpaper, and the ZaPixel app picks up those colours too.

Does ZaPixel have home screen widgets?

Yes, on iPhone. Today's Pick shows the day's wallpaper on your home screen and changes every morning. Shuffle shows a random wallpaper from a category you choose, and can change on its own every hour. To add one, long-press your home screen, tap the + button and search for ZaPixel.

Is ZaPixel available in Arabic?

Yes. The iPhone app is fully translated into Arabic and laid out right to left, category names included, in the same Zain typeface as the English version. It follows your iPhone's language automatically, or you can choose Arabic for ZaPixel alone in the iPhone Settings app under ZaPixel, then Language.

What data does ZaPixel collect?

ZaPixel has no account, so it never asks for your name, email address or a password. Wallpapers you save are kept on your own device. Like most free apps, it collects usage and diagnostic data, such as device type and identifiers, to run the service and its ads. The privacy policy has the details.
