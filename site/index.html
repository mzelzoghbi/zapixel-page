Setting it

AndroidOne tap on Set and it's your wallpaper.

iPhoneApple gives no third-party app a way to set your wallpaper. So ZaPixel doesn't mime it: it drops the wallpaper straight into Photos at full resolution, and spends the screen you'd have wasted on a fake progress bar showing you the picture behind a real lock screen first — clock and all — so you know where the subject lands before you commit.

The colour

AndroidAndroid builds your system palette out of your wallpaper, which puts a wallpaper app upstream of how your whole phone looks. So the Android app takes its colours from the wallpaper you're already using. The blue in that screenshot isn't our brand — it's the phone's.

iPhoneOne violet, holding the tab bar and the save button, and nothing else. Every other colour on screen belongs to a photograph.

What's in it

Built around browsing, not around a grid.

Today's pick

One wallpaper chosen every day and given the top of the screen, so there's always something new worth looking at without going hunting for it.

Browse by category

Nature, Abstract, Minimal, Dark, Cars, Anime and more — ordered by how much is actually in them, not alphabetically. Mark the ones you like as preferred, hide the ones you never want, and your Explore feed follows.

Light, dark, or system
