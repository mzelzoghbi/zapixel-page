Blue River Through the Rocks wallpaper

Blue River Through the Rocks is a free nature and pink aesthetic wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Blue River Through the Rocks in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Blue River Through the Rocks in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
