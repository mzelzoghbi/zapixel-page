Bougainvillea at the Mosque Gate wallpaper

Bougainvillea at the Mosque Gate is a free phone wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

  1. Open Bougainvillea at the Mosque Gate in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Bougainvillea at the Mosque Gate in ZaPixel.
