Captain America and the Stars wallpaper

Captain America and the Stars is a free superheros wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Captain America and the Stars in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Captain America and the Stars in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
