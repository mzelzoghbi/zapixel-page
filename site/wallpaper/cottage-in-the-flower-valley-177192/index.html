Cottage in the Flower Valley wallpaper

Cottage in the Flower Valley is a free abstract and nature wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Cottage in the Flower Valley in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Cottage in the Flower Valley in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
