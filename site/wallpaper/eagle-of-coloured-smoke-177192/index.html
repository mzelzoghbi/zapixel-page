Eagle of Coloured Smoke wallpaper

Eagle of Coloured Smoke is a free abstract and animals wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Eagle of Coloured Smoke in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Eagle of Coloured Smoke in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
