F1 Car in the Red Blur wallpaper

F1 Car in the Red Blur is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open F1 Car in the Red Blur in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open F1 Car in the Red Blur in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
