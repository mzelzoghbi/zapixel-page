Fire and Ice Fluid Art wallpaper

Fire and Ice Fluid Art is a free abstract and pink aesthetic wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Fire and Ice Fluid Art in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Fire and Ice Fluid Art in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
