Ginger Cat Asleep in Daisies wallpaper

Ginger Cat Asleep in Daisies is a free cute and nature wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Ginger Cat Asleep in Daisies in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Ginger Cat Asleep in Daisies in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
