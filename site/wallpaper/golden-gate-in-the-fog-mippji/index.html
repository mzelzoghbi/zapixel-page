Golden Gate in the Fog wallpaper

Golden Gate in the Fog is a free city and dark wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Golden Gate in the Fog in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Golden Gate in the Fog in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
