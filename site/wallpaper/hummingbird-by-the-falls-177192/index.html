Hummingbird by the Falls wallpaper

Hummingbird by the Falls is a free nature and aesthetic wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Hummingbird by the Falls in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Hummingbird by the Falls in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
