Maple Leaf on Walnut wallpaper

Maple Leaf on Walnut is a free nature and minimal wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Maple Leaf on Walnut in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Maple Leaf on Walnut in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
