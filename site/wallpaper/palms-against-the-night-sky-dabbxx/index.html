Palms Against the Night Sky wallpaper

Palms Against the Night Sky is a free nature and aesthetic wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Palms Against the Night Sky in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Palms Against the Night Sky in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
