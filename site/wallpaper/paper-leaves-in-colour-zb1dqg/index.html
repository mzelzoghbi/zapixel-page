Paper Leaves in Colour wallpaper

Paper Leaves in Colour is a free abstract and minimal wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Paper Leaves in Colour in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Paper Leaves in Colour in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
