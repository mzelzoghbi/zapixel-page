Rain on the Swoosh wallpaper

Rain on the Swoosh is a free abstract and aesthetic wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Rain on the Swoosh in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Rain on the Swoosh in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
