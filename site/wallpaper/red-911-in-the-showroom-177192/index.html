Red 911 in the Showroom wallpaper

Red 911 in the Showroom is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Red 911 in the Showroom in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Red 911 in the Showroom in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
