Red Aventador in the Dark wallpaper

Red Aventador in the Dark is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

  1. Open Red Aventador in the Dark in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.

Set it on Android
