Road Through the Pines wallpaper

Road Through the Pines is a free nature and city wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Road Through the Pines in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Road Through the Pines in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
