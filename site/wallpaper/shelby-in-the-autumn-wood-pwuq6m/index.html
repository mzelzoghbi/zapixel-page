Shelby in the Autumn Wood wallpaper

Shelby in the Autumn Wood is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Shelby in the Autumn Wood in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Shelby in the Autumn Wood in ZaPixel.
