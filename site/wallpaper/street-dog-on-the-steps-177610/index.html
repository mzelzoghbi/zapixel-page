Street Dog on the Steps wallpaper

Street Dog on the Steps is a free animals wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Street Dog on the Steps in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Street Dog on the Steps in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
