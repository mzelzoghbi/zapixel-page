Turbo S, Split in Two wallpaper

Turbo S, Split in Two is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Turbo S, Split in Two in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.

Set it on Android
