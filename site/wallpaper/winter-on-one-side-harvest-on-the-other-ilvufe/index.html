Winter on One Side, Harvest on the Other wallpaper

Winter on One Side, Harvest on the Other is a free space wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Winter on One Side, Harvest on the Other in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Winter on One Side, Harvest on the Other in ZaPixel.
