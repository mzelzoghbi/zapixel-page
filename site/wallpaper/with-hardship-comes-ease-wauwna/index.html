With Hardship Comes Ease wallpaper

With Hardship Comes Ease is a free dark and calligraphy wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open With Hardship Comes Ease in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open With Hardship Comes Ease in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.

Tagged arabic characters, black background, heart, inscription, islam, quote.
