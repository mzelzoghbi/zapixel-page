Yellow Roadster by the Wall wallpaper

Yellow Roadster by the Wall is a free cars wallpaper from the ZaPixel catalogue, sized for iPhone and Android lock and home screens.

Free, with no account. The full 4K file is in the app.

Set it on iPhone

  1. Open Yellow Roadster by the Wall in ZaPixel and preview it on the lock screen.
  2. Tap Save — it goes straight to Photos.
  3. In Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Set it on Android

  1. Open Yellow Roadster by the Wall in ZaPixel.
  2. Tap Set, then choose home screen, lock screen or both. Undo is one tap away.
